  • Time: 30 minutes
  • Serves: 12

Ingredients

Ingredients Quantity
butter 1 stick (1/2 cup)
sugar 1/4 cup
flour 3/4 cup
egg 1
vanilla extract 1/2 tsp
baking soda 1/4 tsp
baking powder 1/4 tsp
salt pinch

Instructions

Step 1: preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).

Step 2: mash up butter until smooth

Step 3: mix the sugar and butter until smooth

Step 4: mix in egg and vanilla extract until smooth

Step 5: mix flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt in a separate bowl

Step 6: slowly mix the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients as well as other add-ins you desire until smooth

Step 7: scoop out palm sized balls and flatten on parchment paper on a cookie tray

Step 8: bake for 10-12 minutes
